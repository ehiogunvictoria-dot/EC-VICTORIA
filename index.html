Meet the author

E.C. Victoria

E.C. Victoria is an imaginative author who creates enchanting stories that invite young readers to explore, wonder and dream.

Inspired by the beauty and mystery of the countryside, E.C. Victoria writes fantastical adventures filled with friendship, discovery, curiosity and a touch of magic. Her children's stories are designed to encourage imagination and the joy of reading.

Her creative work also extends to travel journaling, including The Road Log, created for recording journeys, memories and discoveries.
